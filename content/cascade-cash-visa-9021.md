# Cascade Cash Visa: Guide to Benefits

Fictional card created for the BackPocket demo. These terms do not describe any real card.

## §1 Cash Back

1.1 You earn 1.5% cash back on every eligible purchase.

1.2 Cash back is earned as points, and each point is worth 1 cent.

1.3 When a purchase is refunded, the cash back earned on the refunded amount is deducted from your balance.

1.4 Price adjustments or partial refunds of less than $50 do not reduce your cash back.

## §2 Return Protection

2.1 Return Protection may refund you for an eligible item purchased with your Cascade Cash Visa card if the merchant will not accept the return within 90 days of the purchase date.

2.2 Return Protection is limited to $250 per item.

## §3 Important Information

3.1 Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions in this guide. Contact your card issuer to confirm eligibility before relying on any benefit.
