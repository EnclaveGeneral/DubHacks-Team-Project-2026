# Ridgeline Outfitters: Returns, Price Adjustments, and Warranty Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to items purchased from Ridgeline Outfitters online at ridgeline.example.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Purchase date" means the date shown on your order confirmation.

2.2 "Original payment method" means the card or account used to pay for the order.

## 3. Returns

3.1 Unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached may be returned within 30 days of the purchase date for a full refund to the original payment method.

3.2 Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned.

3.3 Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive the returned item.

## 4. Price Adjustments

4.1 We want you to pay the best price for the items you buy from us.

4.2 If an item you purchased is sold by Ridgeline Outfitters at a lower price within 14 days of the purchase date, you may request a one-time price adjustment for the difference.

4.3 Price adjustments apply only to the same item in the same size and color, sold by Ridgeline Outfitters. We do not match prices from other retailers.

4.4 To request a price adjustment, submit the Price Adjustment and Returns form with your order number.

4.5 Price adjustments are refunded to the original payment method.

## 5. Exchanges

5.1 Items may be exchanged for a different size or color within 30 days of the purchase date, subject to availability.

## 6. Warranty

6.1 Ridgeline outerwear is covered by a 1-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship from the purchase date.

6.2 The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidental damage, or alterations.
