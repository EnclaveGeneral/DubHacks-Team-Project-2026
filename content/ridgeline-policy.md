# Ridgeline Outfitters: Return, Price Adjustment, and Warranty Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

1.1 This policy applies to merchandise purchased from Ridgeline Outfitters at ridgeline.example or in the Ridgeline app. Merchandise purchased from other retailers or authorized dealers is subject to that seller's policy.

1.2 We may update this policy from time to time. The policy in effect on your purchase date applies to your order.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Purchase date" means the order date shown on your order confirmation or packing slip.

2.2 "Original form of payment" means the card, gift card, or account used to pay for your order.

## 3. Returns and Refunds

3.1 Merchandise that is unworn, unwashed, and in new condition with original tags attached may be returned within 30 days of the purchase date for a refund to the original form of payment.

3.2 Items marked "Final Sale," gift cards, and personalized items cannot be returned.

3.3 Refunds are issued to the original form of payment within 3-5 business days after we receive and process your return. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

3.4 Returns without an order number or receipt may be refunded as a merchandise credit at the item's current selling price.

## 4. Price Adjustments

4.1 We do not match prices from other retailers, websites, or marketplaces.

4.2 If an item you purchased from Ridgeline Outfitters is offered by us at a lower price, a one-time price adjustment may be requested within 14 days after the purchase date.

4.3 Price adjustments apply only to the identical item, in the same size and color, offered for sale by Ridgeline Outfitters.

4.4 Items purchased with a promotion code or other discount are not eligible for a price adjustment, unless the only discount used was free shipping. Final Sale and clearance items are also excluded.

4.5 Lower prices resulting from typographical errors, limited-quantity offers, or member-only promotions are not eligible.

4.6 To request a price adjustment for an online order, submit a request on our Returns & Refunds page or contact Customer Service with your order number. Your order confirmation, receipt, or packing slip is required.

4.7 Price adjustments are credited to the original form of payment.

## 5. Exchanges

5.1 We do not offer direct exchanges. To exchange an item, return it for a refund and place a new order.

## 6. Warranty

6.1 Ridgeline outerwear is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for one year from the purchase date.

6.2 This warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage caused by accidents, improper care, negligence, or alterations, or the natural breakdown of materials over extended use.

6.3 Covered items will be repaired or, if repair is not possible, replaced or refunded as a merchandise credit, at our discretion.

## 7. Contact Us

7.1 Customer Service is available by chat, by email at help@ridgeline.example, or by phone at 1-800-555-0142, 7 a.m. to 9 p.m. Pacific time.
