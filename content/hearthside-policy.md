# Hearthside Home: Return and Warranty Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

1.1 This policy applies to merchandise purchased from Hearthside Home at hearthside.example or in a Hearthside Home store.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Purchase date" means the order date shown on your order confirmation or packing slip.

## 3. Returns and Refunds

3.1 Merchandise that is unused and in its original packaging may be returned within 30 days of the purchase date for a refund to the original form of payment.

3.2 Bedding, personalized items, and items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned once opened.

3.3 Original shipping charges are not refunded.

## 4. Pricing

4.1 Prices change often. We do not offer price adjustments or price matching after a purchase is made.

## 5. Warranty

5.1 Hearthside Home products are warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for one year from the purchase date.

5.2 This warranty does not cover chips, cracks, or stains caused by normal use or improper care.

## 6. Contact Us

6.1 Customer Service is available by phone at 1-800-555-0177 or by email at help@hearthside.example, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Pacific time.
