# Fjord & Pine Outdoor Co.: Returns, Price Adjustment, and Warranty Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

## 1. About This Policy

1.1 This policy applies to merchandise purchased from Fjord & Pine Outdoor Co. at fjordpine.example or in a Fjord & Pine store. Merchandise purchased from other retailers is subject to that seller's policy.

1.2 The policy in effect on your purchase date applies to your order.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Purchase date" means the order date shown on your order confirmation or receipt.

## 3. Returns and Refunds

3.1 Unused merchandise in its original packaging may be returned within 45 days of the purchase date for a refund to the original form of payment.

3.2 Items marked "Final Sale" and gift cards cannot be returned.

3.3 Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after we receive your return. Original shipping charges are not refunded.

## 4. Price Adjustments

4.1 We do not match prices from other retailers, websites, or marketplaces.

4.2 If an item you purchased from Fjord & Pine Outdoor Co. is offered by us at a lower price, a one-time price adjustment may be requested within 15 days after the purchase date.

4.3 Price adjustments apply only to the identical item, in the same size and color, offered for sale by Fjord & Pine Outdoor Co.

4.4 Items marked "Final Sale" and lower prices resulting from typographical errors are not eligible for a price adjustment.

4.5 To request a price adjustment, chat with us online or email help@fjordpine.example with your order number.

## 5. Warranty

5.1 Fjord & Pine gear is warranted against defects in materials and workmanship for two years from the purchase date.

5.2 This warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, or improper care.

## 6. Contact Us

6.1 Customer Service is available by chat or by email at help@fjordpine.example, 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. Pacific time.
