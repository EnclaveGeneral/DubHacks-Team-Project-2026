# Northwind Rewards Visa: Guide to Benefits

Fictional card created for the BackPocket demo. These terms do not describe any real card.

## §1 Earning Rewards

1.1 You earn 3 points for every $1 spent on eligible purchases.

1.2 Points are worth 1 cent each when redeemed for a statement credit.

1.3 When a purchase is refunded, in full or in part, including any price adjustment, points earned on the refunded amount are deducted from your balance in proportion to the amount refunded.

## §2 Extended Warranty Protection

2.1 Extended Warranty Protection extends the original manufacturer's warranty by 1 additional year on eligible items with an original manufacturer's warranty of 3 years or less.

2.2 The item must be purchased entirely with your Northwind Rewards Visa card.

2.3 Claims must be filed within 60 days of the product failure.

## §3 Important Information

3.1 Coverage is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions in this guide. Contact your card issuer to confirm eligibility before relying on any benefit.
